Saving duties starts before launch

Most duty savings are designed in, not claimed later. When trade gets involved while the product is still a concept, small choices — a material, a factory location, a buy/sell structure — can move the landed cost by percentage points. After launch, the same fixes cost ten times more.

  1. HTS classification preview — classify the concept early so Engineering sees the duty rate attached to each design option, while it's still cheap to change.
  2. Tariff engineering options — flag which features or materials push the product into a higher-duty heading, and what alternatives would land it in a lower one.
  3. AD/CVD scope check — screen the concept against active anti-dumping and countervailing duty orders on the planned materials and components; advise on designing around scope language where commercially viable (and get a scope ruling before scaling).
  4. Country-of-origin planning — advise where the substantial-transformation step should sit to secure favorable origin (and FTA eligibility), before factory contracts are signed.
  5. FTA eligibility mapping — identify which free trade agreements the finished product could qualify for and what input-sourcing constraints that imposes (regional value content, tariff-shift rules).
  6. Valuation structure — set up the buy/sell chain cleanly from the start (first-sale opportunities, assists tracking) rather than reconstructing it years later.
  7. PGA admissibility screen — confirm no hidden import restrictions or licensing on the planned components (FCC, FDA, etc.) that could block or delay launch.
  8. FTZ / drawback fit — flag early whether the product flow suits foreign-trade-zone manufacturing or future duty recovery, since both are harder to retrofit.
  9. Marking and labeling requirements — country-of-origin marking rules affect packaging design; cheaper to build in than to relabel.
  10. Record-keeping hooks — make sure the data trade will need later (BOMs, cost breakdowns, factory records) is captured by the systems being set up now.
The one-liner to sell it internally: every item on this list gets 10× more expensive to fix after launch.
